StoreVitals Privacy Statement

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Publisher: philipsempers.com

StoreVitals is a desktop application that helps Microsoft Store / Partner Center publishers view analytics on their own PC.

What we collect

  • Microsoft account tokens used to call Partner Center / Dev Center APIs on your behalf. Access tokens stay in memory; refresh tokens are stored only in Windows Credential Manager on your device.
  • Analytics data you already own (acquisitions, installs, failures, reviews, etc.), cached locally in a SQLite database on your device.
  • Non-secret settings such as application (client) ID and preferred account audience, stored in local app settings.

What we do not collect

  • We do not receive your Microsoft password.
  • We do not upload your analytics database to a StoreVitals server (there isn’t one for this local app).
  • We do not sell personal data.

Third parties

Sign-in is handled by Microsoft identity services. Partner Center / Dev Center APIs are operated by Microsoft. Their privacy statements apply to those services.

Your choices

You can sign out in StoreVitals to clear refresh tokens from Credential Manager, and you can delete the local SQLite database from your app data folder at any time.
