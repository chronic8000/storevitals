StoreVitals · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Publisher: Philip Sempers

StoreVitals (“the App”) is a Windows desktop application that helps Microsoft Store / Partner Center publishers view their own analytics on their own PC. This policy explains what the App stores locally and what it does not send to us.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the StoreVitals client you install from the Microsoft Store or as a package from the publisher. It does not replace Microsoft’s privacy statements for Microsoft account sign-in, Partner Center, or Dev Center APIs.

Data the App stores on your device

  • Authentication secrets — Entra / Microsoft client secrets and refresh tokens used to call Partner Center / Dev Center APIs. These are stored only in Windows Credential Manager on your device (not in our cloud).
  • Non-secret settings — such as directory (tenant) ID, application (client) ID, and UI preferences, in local app settings.
  • Analytics you already own — acquisitions, installs, revenue, ratings, reviews, health/failure data, and related dimensions — cached in a local SQLite database under your Windows app data folder.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not receive or store your Microsoft account password.
  • We do not operate a StoreVitals analytics backend that receives your Partner Center data.
  • We do not sell personal data or share your local database with third parties for advertising.
  • We do not require you to create a separate StoreVitals online account.

Network requests the App makes

When you sign in or sync, the App contacts Microsoft identity and Partner Center / Dev Center endpoints using credentials you configure. Microsoft’s privacy terms apply to those services. Optional features that open a browser (for example Partner Center review pages) use Microsoft’s websites.

Store license

Packaged Store builds may call Windows Store license APIs on your device to verify purchase / entitlement. That check is performed by Microsoft platform services.

Your choices

  • Sign out in the App to remove stored refresh tokens from Credential Manager.
  • Clear or replace the API client secret in Settings.
  • Delete the App’s local data folder (SQLite cache and settings) via Windows Settings → Apps, or by removing the app data directory after uninstall.
  • Uninstall the App at any time.

Children

The App is intended for developers and publishers managing Microsoft Store products. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
